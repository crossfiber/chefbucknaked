The chef in the apron is a real guy in Key Largo

Buck Donnelly is an award-winning chef and the host of the "Keys' Kitchen" TV show. Hog Rub took him nearly three years to balance, and it is the blend that started the business. Every blend since has been built the same way: a long list of real ingredients instead of a short list and a lot of salt.

You will find him and the family at the Marathon Seafood Festival every year, or on the phone about a custom blend. The label art is by Donald Shank, and the sign-off on every bottle reads the same.
